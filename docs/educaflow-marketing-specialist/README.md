## Product snapshot

**EducaflowOne** is an operations platform for **private tutors and small teaching practices**: students, calendar, lesson planning, finances, student portal, and **materials**. Primary market context: Brazil (`educaflowone.com.br`), Branded as **EDUCAflowOne** in the UI.
